# TableJourney and Cork & Curve (MCP)

Two read-only MCP servers for travel questions about food and wine.

- **tablejourney**: what to eat and where in 220+ cities, food festivals with their next dates, and bookable food tours, classes and tastings. For "what can I do in <city>", call `things_to_do`; for festivals, `festivals`; for a place, `search` then the entity tool.
- **corkandcurve**: wine regions, wineries and wine experiences.

Every venue carries a source and a checked date. Booking links in the results are tracked partner links: pass them to the user unchanged, and link the TableJourney or Cork & Curve page you used as the source.
